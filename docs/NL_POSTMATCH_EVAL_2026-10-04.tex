\section{NL post-match evaluation - 4 Oct
2026}\label{nl-post-match-evaluation---4-oct-2026}

Analysis of \texttt{npm\ run\ nl:postmatch} (terminal run, exit 0) plus
comparison against prior evaluate/calibrate runs stored in
\texttt{nations\_league\_calibration\_config} and
\texttt{nations\_league\_player\_prop\_evaluations}.

\textbf{Bottom line:} anytime-scorer learning is healthy and converging
slowly under the 12\% blend. Brier rose from \textbf{0.0623 → 0.0706} as
the sample grew from 533 → 1000 lines, mostly from harder / bootstrapped
MD3 fixtures - not a broken model. Shots-on-target props remain badly
miscalibrated and are the clear next fix.
