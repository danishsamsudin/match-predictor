MATCH PREVIEW

Two teams in the midst of an eight-match unbeaten streak face off on Friday at Toronto Stadium in Toronto, Ontario as Canada play Bosnia-Herzegovina in the FIFA World Cup group stage.

Both sides are playing their first match of the competition. Canada played out a 1-1 draw in their last match, a June 5 friendly against the Republic of Ireland at Stade Saputo, extending their unbeaten streak to eight games in all competitions.

Bosnia played out a 1-1 draw against Panama in their last game, a June 6 friendly at Energizer Park, improving their unbeaten streak to eight games in all competitions. Nikola Katic was on the scoresheet in the draw.

Jonathan David has caught the eye this year for Canada. He leads the side with two goals. Bosnia's leading lights in attack this year have been Katic, Edin Dzeko, and Haris Tabakovic, who have each netted once.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Canada have won three and drawn three. The Canadian side scored eight and conceded three in that period, scoring first in four of the six contests. They had two first-half goals, while their opponents netted two times in the first 45 minutes. The Bosnian side have won one and drawn five in their last six. The Bosnian side scored seven and conceded five in that stretch, netting first in two of the six. They had two goals in the first half, while their opponents netted three times before the break.
