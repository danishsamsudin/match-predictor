MATCH PREVIEW

Qatar take on an in-form Switzerland side who have not suffered defeat in three matches on Saturday in a FIFA World Cup group stage clash at Santa Clara, California's San Francisco Bay Area Stadium.

It's the first match of the competition for both sides. Qatar played out a 0-0 draw in their last match, a June 6 friendly against El Salvador at BMO Stadium. They have yet to win in three games this year with one draw and one loss.

Switzerland played out a 1-1 draw against Australia in their last game, a June 6 friendly at Snapdragon Stadium, extending their unbeaten streak to three games in all competitions. Dan Ndoye was the only player on the scoresheet in the draw. In 2026, they have won one, drawn two, and lost one.

The previous fixture between the sides saw Qatar triumph 1-0 in a friendly at Stadio di Cornaredo in November 2018. Akram Afif scored for the Qatari side in the 86th minute.

Qatar have yet to score this year. Switzerland have been a threat to opposition teams this year courtesy of Ndoye, who has netted on three occasions, including two match-opening goals.

Qatar have had difficulty in attack in recent play, as they haven't been on the scoresheet in their last 373 minutes of play in all competitions. They last found the net against Syria in a December 2025 Arab Cup match.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Qatar have drawn two and lost three. The Qatari side scored one and conceded six over that stretch, scoring first in one of the six contests. All of their goals came after halftime, while their opponents netted two times in the first 45 minutes. The Swiss side have won two, drawn three and lost one in their last six. The Swiss side scored 13 and conceded eight over that period, netting first in five of the six. Most of their goals (seven) came in the first half, while their opponents netted three times before the break.
