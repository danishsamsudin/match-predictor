MATCH PREVIEW

Czechia have the chance to extend their run without defeat to seven matches when they play Korea Republic in the FIFA World Cup group stage at Zapopan's Estadio Guadalajara on Thursday.

It's the first match of the competition for both sides. Korea Republic's last match saw them triumph 1-0 over El Salvador in a friendly at BYU South Field on June 3. Lee Dong-Gyeong scored in the win. In four games this year, they have two wins and two losses.

Czechia's last game saw them triumph 3-1 over Guatemala in a friendly on June 4 at Sports Illustrated Stadium. Patrik Schick, Tomás Chory, and Denis Visinsky scored in the win.

Korea Republic won the last meeting between the teams, coming out on top 2-1 in a friendly at Fortuna Arena in June 2016. Yoon Bit-Garam scored first for the Korea Republic side in the 26th minute. Suk Hyun-Jun also found the net. Marek Suchy scored the lone goal for the Czechia side in the 46th minute.

Korea Republic have been led by Cho Gue-Sung and Son Heung-Min this year. The two players are the team's joint top scorers with two goals apiece. Ladislav Krejcí and Schick have been Czechia's most prolific players this year, each finding the target twice.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Korea Republic have won four and lost two. The Korea Republic side scored nine and conceded five in that period, scoring first in four of the six contests. They had two first-half goals, while their opponents netted two times in the first 45 minutes. Czech Republic have won four and drawn two in their last six. The Czechia side scored 16 and conceded six over that stretch, netting first in five of the six. Most of their goals (11) came in the first half, while their opponents netted three times before the break.
