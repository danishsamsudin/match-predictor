MATCH PREVIEW

Mexico, unbeaten in eight straight games, will put their run on the line against South Africa in the FIFA World Cup group stage on Thursday at Ciudad de México's Mexico City Stadium.

Both sides are playing their first match of the competition. Mexico's last match saw them triumph 5-1 over Serbia in a friendly at Estadio Nemesio Díez on June 5. Johan Vásquez, Raúl Jiménez, and Luis Chávez netted in the win.

South Africa's last game saw them triumph for the first time this year, winning 1-0 over Jamaica in a friendly on June 6 at Estadio Hidalgo. In 2026, they have won one, drawn two, and lost two.

The clubs played out a 1-1 draw in their last meeting in June 2010.

Mexico have been led by Vásquez and Brian Gutiérrez this year. The two players are the team's joint top scorers with two goals apiece.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Mexico have won four and drawn two. The Mexican side scored 13 and conceded two in that stretch, scoring first in four of the six contests. A majority of their goals (seven) came in the first half, while their opponents netted once in the first 45 minutes. The South African side have won two, drawn two and lost two in their last six. The South African side scored seven and conceded seven in that stretch, netting first in one of the six. They had only one goal in the first half, while their opponents netted three times before the break.
