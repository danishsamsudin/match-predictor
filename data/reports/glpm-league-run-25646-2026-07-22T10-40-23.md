## What the model means as a whole

**Layer 1 — Data:** SportMonks match stats (with transparent proxies where needed).

**Layer 2 — Feature engineering:** Raw stats become per-match features (shots, xG, PPDA, progression, etc.).

**Layer 3 — Nested ML (seven engines):** For each skill, the pipeline:
1. Strips home/rest/congestion effects and scales for opponent strength,
2. Learns six **component** models (specialist sub-skills),
3. Combines components into three **domain** models,
4. Combines domains into one **primary** rating,
5. Maps latent scores to the universal 0–100 GLPM scale.

**Layer 4 — Prediction (fixed equations):** Primary ratings assemble into a Rating Vector. The xG engine applies locked interaction weights (Attack–Defence 40%, Finishing–GK 25%, Build-Up–Pressing 20%, Possession–Pressing 15%) and Dixon–Coles math to produce match probabilities.

**This run:** Learned weights are effectively unchanged — new data did not materially shift the fitted regressors. Team ratings may still nudge from the latest matches entering the training set.
