- **Baseline goals (μ)** (+5.0%): The model's neutral expected goals per team before strength gaps are applied — think of it as the typical scoring rate in this tournament. Future matches get slightly higher expected goal totals; overs and BTTS lean up where teams are evenly matched.
- **Momentum index weight** (-1.3%): How much the momentum index (results trajectory) shifts strength beyond xG-Elo. Results streaks are discounted; expected goals drive more of the call.
- **FIFA ranking weight** (-1.3%): How much official FIFA rank gap nudges predictions. FIFA rank is mostly ignored; model leans on xG and tournament data.
- **xG-Elo weight** (-1.3%): How much our xG-based Elo rating gap moves the prediction. Other signals (talent, FIFA rank, tournament form) matter relatively more.
- **In-tournament xG form weight** (-1.3%): How much each team's xG created/conceded in this World Cup adjusts the next match. Structural ratings change more slowly; one-off xG swings matter less.
- **Pre-tournament form weight** (-1.3%): How much qualifying / pre-WC form shifts the baseline strength gap. In-tournament evidence and xG-Elo dominate over pre-tournament runs.
- **Momentum sensitivity** (+1.0%): How much recent winning/losing streaks nudge the next prediction beyond structural strength. Hot teams get a bigger boost and cold teams a bigger penalty in upcoming fixtures.
- **Strength gap exponent** (-0.6%): How sharply a favourite's advantage turns into extra xG. Higher values make gaps between teams matter more. Underdogs keep more chance; tight games and draws stay relatively more likely.
