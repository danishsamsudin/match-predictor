MATCH PREVIEW

The United States face Australia on Friday at Seattle Stadium in a meeting of teams who boast perfect starts early in the FIFA World Cup group stage.

The United States earned a 4-1 victory over Paraguay in their opening World Cup group stage match on June 12. Folarin Balogun caught the eye with a brace. In five games this year, they have two wins and three losses.

Australia won their opening World Cup group stage game on June 13, beating Türkiye 2-0 at BC Place Vancouver. Nestory Irankunda and Connor Metcalfe scored in the win. In 2026, they have won three, drawn one, and lost one.

The United States and Australia are first and second in the group, respectively, level on three points. Elsewhere in the group, Türkiye are sitting third on zero points. Paraguay are fourth on zero points.

The United States won the last meeting between the sides, coming out on top 2-1 in a friendly at Dick's Sporting Goods Park last October. Haji Wright led the USA side with goals in the 33rd and 51st minute. Jordan Bos scored the only goal for the Australian side in the 19th minute.

Balogun leads the United States in World Cup scoring after netting a brace against Paraguay. With goals against Türkiye, Irankunda and Metcalfe are the early leading World Cup scorers for Australia.

In their last six matches in all competitions, the United States have won three and lost three. The USA side scored 15 and conceded 13 in that period, scoring first in four of the six contests. A majority of their goals (11) came in the first half, while their opponents netted five times in the first 45 minutes. The Australian side have won three, drawn one and lost two in their last six. The Australian side scored nine and conceded six in that stretch, netting first in three of the six. They had two goals in the first half, while their opponents netted two times before the break.
