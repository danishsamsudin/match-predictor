MATCH PREVIEW

Two teams looking for their first win in the FIFA World Cup group stage will clash at Estadio Monterrey on Saturday, as Tunisia take on Japan.

Tunisia were defeated in their opening World Cup group stage match, falling 5-1 against Sweden on June 14. Omar Rekik was the only player to score in the loss. They are winless in their last four games in all competitions. In six games this year, they have one win, one draw, and four losses.

Japan played out a 2-2 draw against the Netherlands in their opening World Cup game, a June 14 group stage clash at Dallas Stadium, extending their unbeaten streak to eight. Keito Nakamura and Daichi Kamada netted in the draw.

Japan are second in the group on one point, while Tunisia's zero points place them fourth. Atop the group on three points are Sweden. The Netherlands are third on one point.

When the two sides last met in action, Japan were victorious 2-0 in a friendly at NOEVIR Stadium Kobe in October 2023. Kyogo Furuhashi opened the scoring for the Japanese side in the 43rd minute. Junya Ito also was on the scoresheet.

Rekik has the early World Cup scoring lead for Tunisia after netting against Sweden. With goals against the Netherlands, Nakamura and Kamada are the early leading World Cup scorers for Japan.

Japan have scored in each of their last eight matches in all competitions. They haven't been shut out since a friendly with United States in September 2025.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Tunisia have won one, drawn two and lost three. The Tunisian side scored three and conceded 12 over that stretch, scoring first in two of the six contests. A majority of their goals (two) came in the first half, while their opponents netted three times in the first 45 minutes. Japan have won five and drawn one in their last six. The Japanese side scored 10 and conceded two in that period, netting first in five of the six. They had three goals in the first half, while their opponents failed to find the net before the break.
