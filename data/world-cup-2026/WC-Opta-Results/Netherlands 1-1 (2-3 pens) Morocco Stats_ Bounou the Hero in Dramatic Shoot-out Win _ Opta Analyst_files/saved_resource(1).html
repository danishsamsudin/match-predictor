MATCH PREVIEW

The Netherlands play Morocco on Monday for a FIFA World Cup Round of 32 clash at Estadio Monterrey.

The Netherlands earned a 3-1 victory over Tunisia in their last group stage match on June 25. Brian Brobbey and Jan Paul van Hecke netted in the win. They are unbeaten in their last four games in all competitions. In seven games this year, they have four wins, two draws, and one loss.

Morocco won their last group stage game on June 24, beating Haiti 4-2 at Atlanta Stadium, improving their unbeaten streak to 32. Four players scored in the win.

Both sides finished on seven points in their respective groups. The Netherlands placed first, while Morocco were second in their group.

The Netherlands won the last meeting between the sides, triumphing 2-1 in a friendly at Stade d'Agadir in May 2017. Quincy Promes struck first for the Dutch side in the 22nd minute. Vincent Janssen also was on the scoresheet. Moubarak Boussoufa scored Morocco's lone goal in the 72nd minute.

The Netherlands have been led by Brobbey in World Cup play. The striker leads the team with three goals, which is tied for the fifth highest in the competition's scoring charts, one of those having been the tone-setting first of the match. Morocco's most productive player in attack has been Ismael Saibari, who has netted on three occasions in the current World Cup campaign, including two match-opening goals. The attacking midfielder is tied for fifth among the competition's top scorers.

Both sides are on World Cup scoring streaks, as each have found the net in every match in the competition this season.

In their last six matches in all competitions, the Netherlands have won three, drawn two and lost one. The Dutch side scored 13 and conceded seven in that period, scoring first in five of the six contests. They had six first-half goals, while their opponents netted once in the first 45 minutes. Morocco have won four and drawn two in their last six. The Moroccan side scored 16 and conceded four over that period, netting first in four of the six. They had seven goals in the first half, while their opponents netted three times before the break.
