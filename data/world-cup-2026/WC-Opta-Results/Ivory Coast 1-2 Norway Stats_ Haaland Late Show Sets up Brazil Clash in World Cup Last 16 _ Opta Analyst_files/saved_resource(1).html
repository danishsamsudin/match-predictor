MATCH PREVIEW

Côte d'Ivoire face Norway on Tuesday for a FIFA World Cup Round of 32 clash at Dallas Stadium.

Côte d'Ivoire defeated Curaçao 2-0 in their last group stage match on June 25. Nicolas Pépé was the only player on the scoresheet, netting a brace. In eight games this year, they have six wins and two losses.

Norway's last group stage game saw them beaten for the first time in the competition this year 4-1 by France on June 26 at Boston Stadium. Thelo Aasgaard was the sole player to net in the loss. In 2026, they have won three, drawn two, and lost two.

Both sides finished second in their respective groups on six points.

Pépé has caught the eye for Côte d'Ivoire in World Cup play. He is their leading scorer with two goals, one of those having been the critical first of the match. Erling Haaland has been the most prolific player for Norway, scoring on four occasions in the current World Cup campaign, including one match-opening goal. He is tied for second in the competition's scoring charts.

Both sides are on World Cup scoring streaks, as each have been on the scoresheet in every match in the competition this season.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Côte d'Ivoire have won five and lost one. The Ivorian side scored 11 and conceded three in that stretch, scoring first in five of the six contests. They had five first-half goals, while their opponents netted once in the first 45 minutes. The Norwegian side have won three, drawn two and lost one in their last six. The Norwegian side scored 12 and conceded nine in that stretch, netting first in three of the six. Most of their goals (seven) came in the first half, while their opponents netted five times before the break.
