Haiti play Morocco for a FIFA World Cup group stage match at Atlanta Stadium on Wednesday with Morocco looking to clinch a place in the Round of 32.

Haiti slumped 3-0 against Brazil in their last group stage game, a June 19 clash at Philadelphia Stadium, their third consecutive defeat. In 2026, they have won one, drawn one, and lost four.

Morocco are second in the group on four points, while Haiti's zero points place them fourth. Morocco are currently in position for a place in the Round of 32, while Haiti are in line to be eliminated.

Brazil are leading the group on four points and are well-positioned to advance to the Round of 32. Scotland are still in contention for a round of 32 place, sitting third on three points.

Morocco have been led by Saibari in World Cup play. He leads the team with two goals, which is tied for the fourth highest in the competition. Haiti are still looking for their first goal of this World Cup campaign.

Morocco have scored in each of their last eight contests in all competitions. They have netted in every match since a CAF Africa Cup of Nations meeting with Nigeria in January. Haiti, meanwhile, are scoreless in their last 254 minutes of play. They last found the net against Peru in a 6 June friendly.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Morocco have won four and drawn two. The Moroccan side scored 14 and conceded three over that stretch, scoring first in five of the six contests.
