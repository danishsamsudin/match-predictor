MATCH PREVIEW

Czechia play Mexico for a FIFA World Cup group stage match at Mexico City Stadium on Wednesday with the Mexican side having already secured a place in the Round of 32.

Czechia earned a point for the first time in the competition this season in their last group stage match, drawing 1-1 against South Africa on June 18. Michal Sadílek was the only player to net in the draw. In six games this year, they have four wins, one draw, and one loss.

Mexico's last group stage game saw them triumph 1-0 over Korea Republic on June 18 at Estadio Guadalajara, extending their unbeaten streak to 10. Luis Romo scored in the win.

Mexico are top of the group on six points. Czechia's one point places them third, currently out of the knockout round places but still on for a place in the round of 32.

Korea Republic are second in the group on three points, well-positioned to advance to the Round of 32. South Africa are facing elimination, sitting fourth on one point.

The previous encounter between the sides saw Czechia emerge victorious 2-1 in February 2000.

Two players who have caught the eye for Czechia in World Cup play are Ladislav Krejcí and Sadílek. The duo are the team's joint top scorers with one goal. Krejcí and Sadílek also share the same number of match-opening goals with one each. Mexico have been helped by Romo, Julián Quiñones, and Raúl Jiménez, who have each scored once in the current World Cup campaign.

Czechia have scored in nine straight matches in all competitions. They have scored in every match since a World Cup Qualifiers meeting with Croatia in October 2025.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Czechia have won two, drawn three and lost one. The Czechia side scored 11 and conceded nine over that stretch, scoring first in five of the six contests. A majority of their goals (six) came in the first half, while their opponents netted three times in the first 45 minutes. Mexico have won five and drawn one in their last six. The Mexican side scored 12 and conceded two in that stretch, netting first in five of the six. They had six goals in the first half, while their opponents netted one time before the break.
