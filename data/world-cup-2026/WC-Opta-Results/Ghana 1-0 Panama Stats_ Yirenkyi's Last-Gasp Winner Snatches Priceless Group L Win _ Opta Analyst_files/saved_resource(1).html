Stuttering Ghana are winless in six consecutive games going into their FIFA World Cup group stage match against Panama on Wednesday at Toronto Stadium.

Both sides are playing their first match of the competition. Ghana played out a 1-1 draw in their last match, a June 2 friendly against Wales at Cardiff City Stadium, stretching their winless streak to six games in all competitions. Caleb Yirenkyi was the only player to net in the draw.

Panama played out a 1-1 draw against Bosnia-Herzegovina in their last game, a June 6 friendly at Energizer Park. Jiovany Ramos was the lone player to score in the draw. In 2026, they have won two, drawn three, and lost two.

Ghana have been indebted to Abdul Fatawu, Jordan Ayew, and Yirenkyi in 2026. They are the team's joint top scorers with one goal apiece. Ramos and Kadir Barría have been Panama's most prolific players this year, each finding the target twice.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Ghana have drawn one and lost five. The Ghanaian side scored three and conceded 13 in that period, scoring first in one of the six contests. All of their goals came after halftime, while their opponents netted four times in the first 45 minutes. The Panamanian side have won two, drawn two and lost two in their last six. The Panamanian side scored 10 and conceded 12 over that period, netting first in three of the six. They had five goals in the first half, while their opponents netted three times before the break.
