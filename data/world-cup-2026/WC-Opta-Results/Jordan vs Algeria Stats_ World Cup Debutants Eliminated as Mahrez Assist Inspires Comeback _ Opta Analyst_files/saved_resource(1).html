Jordan take on Algeria on Monday at San Francisco Bay Area Stadium in a meeting of teams who are looking for their first win of the FIFA World Cup group stage.

Jordan and Algeria are third and fourth in the group, respectively, level on zero points. With three points, Argentina are leading the group. Austria are second, also on three points.

Olwan leads Jordan in World Cup scoring after netting against Austria. Algeria are still looking for their first goal of this season's World Cup.
