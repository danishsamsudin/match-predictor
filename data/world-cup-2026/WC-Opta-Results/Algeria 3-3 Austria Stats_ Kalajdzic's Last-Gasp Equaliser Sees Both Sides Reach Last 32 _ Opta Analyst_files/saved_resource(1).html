Algeria take on Austria for a FIFA World Cup group stage match at Kansas City Stadium on Saturday with the Austrian side looking to secure a place in the Round of 32.

Algeria were victorious for the first time in the competition this season when they defeated Jordan 2-1 in their last group stage match on June 22. Nadhir Benbouali and Amine Gouiri netted in the win. In eight games this year, they have five wins, one draw, and two losses.

Austria suffered a first competition loss of the year in a 2-0 defeat against Argentina in their last group stage game, a June 22 clash at Dallas Stadium. In 2026, they have won four and lost one.

Austria and Algeria are second and third in the group, respectively, level on three points. Austria are currently in line for the Round of 32, while Algeria are out of the knockout round places but still on for a place in the round of 32.

Atop the group on six points are Argentina, who have clinched their place in the Round of 32. Jordan are facing elimination, sitting fourth on zero points.

Austria won the previous encounter between the sides, coming out on top 2-0 in June 1982.

Gouiri and Benbouali have proved attacking threats for Algeria in World Cup play. The pair are the team's joint top scorers with one goal. Austria have been thankful to Romano Schmid and Marko Arnautovic, who have each netted once in this World Cup campaign.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Algeria have won four, drawn one and lost one. The Algerian side scored 14 and conceded four over that period, scoring first in three of the six contests. They had four first-half goals, while their opponents netted two times in the first 45 minutes. The Austrian side have won four, drawn one and lost one in their last six.
