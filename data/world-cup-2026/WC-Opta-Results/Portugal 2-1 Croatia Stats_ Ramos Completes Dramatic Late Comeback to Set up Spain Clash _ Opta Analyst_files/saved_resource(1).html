Portugal take on Croatia on Thursday for a FIFA World Cup Round of 32 clash at Toronto Stadium.

Both sides finished second in their respective groups. Croatia earned six points in group play, while Portugal had five points.

The previous fixture between the sides saw them play out a 1-1 draw in an UEFA Nations League League A match at Stadion Poljud in November 2024. João Félix scored Portugal's lone goal in the 33rd minute. Josko Gvardiol scored Croatia's only goal in the 65th minute.

Cristiano Ronaldo has caught the eye for Portugal in World Cup play. He leads the team with two goals, one of which has been the tone-setting first of the match. Croatia have been led by five players who have each netted once in this season's World Cup.

The Croatian side have scored in each of their three World Cup contests this season.
