MATCH PREVIEW

Brazil play Japan on Monday for a FIFA World Cup Round of 32 clash at Houston Stadium.

Brazil defeated Scotland 3-0 in their last group stage match on June 24. Vinícius Júnior led the team with two goals. They are undefeated in their last six games in all competitions. In seven games this year, they have five wins, one draw, and one loss.

Japan played out a 1-1 draw against Sweden in their last game, a June 25 group stage clash at Dallas Stadium, improving their unbeaten streak to 10. Daizen Maeda scored in the draw.

Brazil finished first in their group on seven points, while Japan's five points placed them second.

When the two sides last met in action, Japan came out on top 3-2 in a friendly at Ajinomoto Stadium last October. Takumi Minamino opened the scoring for the Japanese side in the 52nd minute. Keito Nakamura and Ayase Ueda also were on the scoresheet. Paulo Henrique and Gabriel Martinelli were on target for the Brazilian side.

Júnior has caught the eye for Brazil in World Cup play. He is the side's top scorer with four goals, which is tied for the second highest in the competition's scoring charts, one of those having been the tone-setting first of the match. Daichi Kamada and Ueda have been Japan's most prolific players in this World Cup campaign, each scoring twice.

Both sides have found success in the final third in World Cup play, as each have found the net in every match in the competition this season.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Brazil have won five and drawn one. The Brazilian side scored 18 and conceded five over that period, scoring first in five of the six contests. A majority of their goals (10) came in the first half, while their opponents netted three times in the first 45 minutes. The Japanese side have won four and drawn two in their last six. The Japanese side scored 10 and conceded three over that period, netting first in five of the six. They had three goals in the first half, while their opponents failed to find the net before the break.
