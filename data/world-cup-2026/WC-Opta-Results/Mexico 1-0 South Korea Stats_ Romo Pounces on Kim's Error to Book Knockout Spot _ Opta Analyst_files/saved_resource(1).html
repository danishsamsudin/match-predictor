MATCH PREVIEW

Mexico face Korea Republic on Thursday at Estadio Guadalajara in a clash of teams who are yet to drop points early in the FIFA World Cup group stage.

Mexico's opening World Cup group stage match saw them triumph 2-0 over South Africa on June 11. Julián Quiñones and Raúl Jiménez scored in the win. They are unbeaten in their last nine games in all competitions.

Korea Republic earned a 2-1 victory over Czechia in their opening World Cup group stage game, a June 11 clash at Estadio Guadalajara, their third straight victory. Hwang In-Beom and Oh Hyeon-Gyu were on the scoresheet in the win. In 2026, they have won three and lost two.

Mexico and Korea Republic are first and second in the group, respectively, level on three points. Elsewhere in the group, Czechia are sitting third on zero points. South Africa are fourth on zero points.

The previous fixture between the teams saw them play out a 2-2 draw in a friendly at GEODIS Park last September. Jiménez and Santiago Giménez were on the scoresheet for the Mexican side. Son Heung-Min and Oh found the net for the Korea Republic side.

Quiñones and Jiménez share the early World Cup scoring lead for Mexico after each netting against South Africa. With goals against Czechia, Hwang and Oh are the early leading World Cup scorers for Korea Republic.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Mexico have won four and drawn two. The Mexican side scored 11 and conceded two in that period, scoring first in four of the six contests. A majority of their goals (six) came in the first half, while their opponents netted once in the first 45 minutes. The Korea Republic side have won four and lost two in their last six. The Korea Republic side scored nine and conceded six in that period, netting first in three of the six. They had two goals in the first half, while their opponents netted two times before the break.
