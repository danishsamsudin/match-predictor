MATCH PREVIEW

Sweden will look to protect their perfect start early in the FIFA World Cup group stage when they play the Netherlands at Houston Stadium on Saturday.

The Netherlands earned a point in their opening World Cup group stage match, drawing 2-2 against Japan on June 14. Virgil van Dijk and Crysencio Summerville scored in the draw. In five games this year, they have two wins, two draws, and one loss.

Sweden defeated Tunisia 5-1 in their opening World Cup group stage game, a June 14 clash at Estadio Monterrey. Yasin Ayari led the team with a brace. In 2026, they have won three, drawn one, and lost one.

Sweden lead the group on three points, while the Netherlands' one point places them third. Elsewhere in the group, Japan are sitting second on one point. Tunisia are fourth on zero points.

The previous fixture between the teams saw the Netherlands triumph 2-0 in an UEFA World Cup Qualifiers 1st Round match at Johan Cruijff ArenA in October 2017. Arjen Robben led Netherlands with two goals, scoring in the 16th and 40th minute.

van Dijk and Summerville share the early World Cup scoring lead for the Netherlands after each netting against Japan. With a brace against Tunisia, Ayari is the early leading World Cup scorer for Sweden.

In their last six matches in all competitions, the Netherlands have won three, drawn two and lost one. The Dutch side scored 11 and conceded six over that stretch, scoring first in four of the six contests. They had four first-half goals, while their opponents netted two times in the first 45 minutes. The Swedish side have won three, drawn two and lost one in their last six. The Swedish side scored 15 and conceded 10 in that stretch, netting first in three of the six. They had five goals in the first half, while their opponents netted six times before the break.
