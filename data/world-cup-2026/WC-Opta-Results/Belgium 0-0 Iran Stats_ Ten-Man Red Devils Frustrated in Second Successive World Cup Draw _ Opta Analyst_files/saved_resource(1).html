MATCH PREVIEW

Belgium take on IR Iran on Sunday at Los Angeles Stadium in a meeting of teams who are looking for their first win of the FIFA World Cup group stage.

Belgium earned a point in their opening World Cup group stage match, drawing 1-1 against Egypt on June 15. They are undefeated in their last 14 games in all competitions.

Iran played out a 2-2 draw against New Zealand in their opening World Cup game, a June 15 group stage clash at Los Angeles Stadium, extending their unbeaten streak to four. Ramin Rezaeian and Mohammad Mohebbi were on the scoresheet in the draw. In 2026, they have won three, drawn one, and lost one.

IR Iran and Belgium are second and third in the group, respectively, level on one point. Leading the group on one point are New Zealand. Egypt are fourth, also on one point.

With goals against New Zealand, Rezaeian and Mohebbi are the early leading World Cup scorers for Iran.

Belgium have scored in eight straight matches in all competitions. They haven't been shut out since a World Cup Qualifiers meeting with North Macedonia in October 2025.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Belgium have won four and drawn two. The Belgian side scored 21 and conceded four over that period, scoring first in three of the six contests. They had six first-half goals, while their opponents netted three times in the first 45 minutes. The Iranian side have won three, drawn one and lost two in their last six. The Iranian side scored 13 and conceded five in that period, netting first in one of the six. They had five goals in the first half, while their opponents netted three times before the break.
