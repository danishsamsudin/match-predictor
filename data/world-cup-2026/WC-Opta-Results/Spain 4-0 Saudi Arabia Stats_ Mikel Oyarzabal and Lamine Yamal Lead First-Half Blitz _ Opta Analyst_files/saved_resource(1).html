MATCH PREVIEW

Two teams looking for their first win in the FIFA World Cup group stage will clash at Atlanta Stadium on Sunday, as Spain face Saudi Arabia.

Spain played out a 0-0 draw in their opening World Cup group stage match against Cabo Verde on June 15. They are undefeated in their last 11 games in all competitions.

Saudi Arabia played out a 1-1 draw against Uruguay in their opening World Cup game, a June 15 group stage clash at Miami Stadium, improving their unbeaten streak to three. Abdulelah Al Amri netted in the draw. In 2026, they have won one, drawn two, and lost three.

Saudi Arabia and Spain are second and third in the group, respectively, level on one point. Leading the group are Uruguay on one point. Cabo Verde are fourth, also on one point.

In the previous encounter between the clubs in September 2012, it was Spain who emerged victorious 5-0.

Spain have yet to find the net in the World Cup this season. With a goal against Uruguay, Al Amri is the early leading World Cup scorer for Saudi Arabia.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Spain have won two and drawn four. The Spanish side scored nine and conceded four over that stretch, scoring first in four of the six contests. A majority of their goals (six) came in the first half, while their opponents netted two times in the first 45 minutes. The Saudi side have won one, drawn two and lost three in their last six. The Saudi side scored six and conceded nine over that stretch, netting first in three of the six. They had three goals in the first half, while their opponents netted four times before the break.
