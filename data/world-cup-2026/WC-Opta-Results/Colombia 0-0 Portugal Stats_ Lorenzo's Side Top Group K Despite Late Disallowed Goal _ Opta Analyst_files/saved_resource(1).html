MATCH PREVIEW

Portugal play Colombia for a FIFA World Cup group stage match at Miami Stadium on Saturday with Colombia having already clinched a place in the Round of 32 and the Portuguese side looking to join them.

Colombia earned a 1-0 victory over Congo DR in their last group stage match on June 23. Daniel Muñoz was on the scoresheet in the win. They have four straight wins in all competitions. In six games this year, they have four wins and two losses.

Portugal won for the first time in the competition this year in their last group stage game on June 23, beating Uzbekistan 5-0 at Houston Stadium, extending their unbeaten streak to seven. Cristiano Ronaldo netted two to lead the scoring.

Colombia lead the group on six points. Portugal's four points place them second, currently in position to join Colombia in the Round of 32.

Elsewhere in the group, Congo DR are third on one point and still in contention for a round of 32 place. Uzbekistan are facing elimination, sitting fourth on zero points.

Colombia have been led by Muñoz in World Cup play. The defender leads the team with two goals. Ronaldo has been the leading attacking threat for Portugal, scoring twice in this season's World Cup, including one match-opening goal.

Colombia have scored in eight straight matches in all competitions. They haven't been shut out since a friendly with Canada in October 2025.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Colombia have won four and lost two. The Colombian side scored 11 and conceded seven in that period, scoring first in five of the six contests. They had five first-half goals, while their opponents netted five times in the first 45 minutes. The Portuguese side have won four and drawn two in their last six. The Portuguese side scored 12 and conceded three in that period, netting first in five of the six. They had six goals in the first half, while their opponents netted two times before the break.
