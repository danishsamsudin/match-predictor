MATCH PREVIEW

Brazil face Haiti on Friday at Philadelphia Stadium in a clash of teams who have yet to earn a win early in the FIFA World Cup group stage.

Brazil played out a 1-1 draw in their opening World Cup group stage match against Morocco on June 13. Vinícius Júnior scored in the draw. They are undefeated in their last four games in all competitions. In five games this year, they have three wins, one draw, and one loss.

Haiti slumped 1-0 against Scotland in their opening World Cup group stage game, a June 13 clash at Boston Stadium. In 2026, they have won one, drawn one, and lost three.

Brazil are third in the group on one point, while Haiti's zero points place them fourth. With three points, Scotland are sitting atop the group. Morocco are second on one point.

The previous fixture between the teams saw Brazil win 7-1 in a CONMEBOL Copa America Group Stage match at Camping World Stadium in June 2016. Philippe Coutinho led the Brazilian side with goals in the 14th, 29th, and 92nd minute. Renato Augusto also found the net twice. James Marcelin scored Haiti's only goal in the 70th minute.

Júnior leads Brazil in World Cup scoring after netting against Morocco. Haiti are still looking for their first goal of this World Cup campaign.

Brazil have scored in nine straight contests in all competitions. They have hit the back of the net in every match since a World Cup Qualifiers meeting with Bolivia in September 2025.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Brazil have won three, drawn two and lost one. The Brazilian side scored 14 and conceded eight over that stretch, scoring first in three of the six contests. They had six first-half goals, while their opponents netted five times in the first 45 minutes. The Haitian side have won two, drawn one and lost three in their last six. The Haitian side scored eight and conceded five over that stretch, netting first in three of the six. They had four goals in the first half, while their opponents netted two times before the break.
