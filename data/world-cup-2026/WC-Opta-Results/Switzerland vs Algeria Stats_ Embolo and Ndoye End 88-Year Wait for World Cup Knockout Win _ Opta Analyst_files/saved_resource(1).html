MATCH PREVIEW

Switzerland play Algeria on Thursday for a FIFA World Cup Round of 32 clash at BC Place Vancouver.

Switzerland's last group stage match saw them triumph 2-1 over Canada on June 24. Rubén Vargas and Johan Manzambi scored in the win. They are undefeated in their last six games in all competitions. In seven games this year, they have three wins, three draws, and one loss.

Algeria played out a 3-3 draw against Austria in their last game, a June 27 group stage clash at Kansas City Stadium. Riyad Mahrez caught the eye with a brace. In 2026, they have won five, drawn two, and lost two.

Switzerland finished first in their group on seven points, while Algeria's four points placed them third.

Manzambi has caught the eye for Switzerland in World Cup play. He is the team's top scorer with three goals, one of those having been the crucial first of the match. Algeria have been a threat in the final third courtesy of Mahrez, who has scored twice in this season's World Cup.

Switzerland have scored in each of their three World Cup contests this season.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Switzerland have won three and drawn three. The Swiss side scored 12 and conceded five in that stretch, scoring first in five of the six contests. They had five first-half goals, while their opponents failed to find the net in the first 45 minutes. The Algerian side have won three, drawn two and lost one in their last six. The Algerian side scored 10 and conceded seven in that period, netting first in two of the six. They had two goals in the first half, while their opponents netted three times before the break.
