MATCH PREVIEW

Stuttering Tunisia are winless in three consecutive games going into their FIFA World Cup group stage match against Sweden on Sunday at Estadio Monterrey.

It's the first match of the competition for both sides. Sweden played out a 2-2 draw in their last match, a June 4 friendly against Greece at Strawberry Arena. Viktor Gyökeres and Gustaf Nilsson were on the scoresheet in the draw. In four games this year, they have two wins, one draw, and one loss.

Tunisia were defeated in their last game, losing 5-0 against Belgium in a friendly at Stade Roi Baudouin on June 6. In 2026, they have won one, drawn one, and lost three.

Tunisia won the last encounter between the clubs, emerging victorious 1-0 in February 2003.

Gyökeres has caught the eye this year for Sweden. He leads the side with five goals. Tunisia's leading attacking threats this year have been Firas Chaouat and Sebastian Tounekti, who have each netted once.

Tunisia have struggled to find success in the final third of late, as they have gone 353 minutes in all competitions without scoring. They last found the net against Haiti in a March friendly.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Sweden have won two, drawn two and lost two. The Swedish side scored 11 and conceded 13 in that period, scoring first in two of the six contests. They had four first-half goals, while their opponents netted six times in the first 45 minutes. The Tunisian side have won one, drawn three and lost two in their last six. The Tunisian side scored three and conceded eight in that period, netting first in three of the six. Most of their goals (two) came in the first half, while their opponents netted only one before the break.
