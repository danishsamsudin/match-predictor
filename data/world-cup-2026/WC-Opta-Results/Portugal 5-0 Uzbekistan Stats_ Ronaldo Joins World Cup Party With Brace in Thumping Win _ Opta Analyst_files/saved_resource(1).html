MATCH PREVIEW

Portugal face Uzbekistan on Tuesday at Houston Stadium in a meeting of teams who have yet to earn a win early in the FIFA World Cup group stage.

Portugal played out a 1-1 draw in their opening World Cup group stage match against Congo DR on June 17. João Neves scored in the draw. They are undefeated in their last six games in all competitions.

Uzbekistan slumped 3-1 against Colombia in their opening World Cup group stage game, a June 17 clash at Mexico City Stadium, their third straight defeat. Abbosbek Fayzullaev was the only player on the scoresheet in the loss. In 2026, they have won two and lost three.

Portugal are third in the group on one point, while Uzbekistan's zero points place them fourth. Colombia are sitting first in the group on three points. Congo DR are second on one point.

Neves leads Portugal in World Cup scoring after netting against Congo DR. With a goal against Colombia, Fayzullaev is the early leading World Cup scorer for Uzbekistan.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Portugal have won four and drawn two. The Portuguese side scored 16 and conceded four in that period, scoring first in five of the six contests. They had eight first-half goals, while their opponents netted three times in the first 45 minutes. Uzbekistan have won three and lost three in their last six. The Uzbek side scored five and conceded eight over that stretch, failing to score first in any of the six. All of their goals came after halftime, while their opponents netted two times before the break.
