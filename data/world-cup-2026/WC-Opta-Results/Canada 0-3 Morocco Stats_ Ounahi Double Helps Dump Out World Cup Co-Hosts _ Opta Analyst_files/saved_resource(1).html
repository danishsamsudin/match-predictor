MATCH PREVIEW

Canada face Morocco on Saturday for a FIFA World Cup Round of 16 clash at Houston Stadium.

Canada won their last World Cup match, beating South Africa 1-0 in the round of 32 on June 28. Stephen Eustaquio was the only player to score in the win. In nine games this year, they have four wins, four draws, and one loss.

Morocco won their World Cup round of 32 game 1-1 (3-2) on penalties against the Netherlands on June 29. Neither team scored in the additional period, sending the sides to spot-kicks. Issa Diop was the sole player to net in the win. They are unbeaten in 33 in a row.

When the two sides last met in action, Morocco emerged victorious 2-1 in a 2022 World Cup match at Al Thumama Stadium in December 2022. Hakim Ziyech struck first for Morocco in the fourth minute. Youssef En-Nesyri also was on the scoresheet. Canada's only goal came courtesy of Morocco's Nayef Aguerd putting the ball in his own net.

Canada have been led by Jonathan David in World Cup play. He leads the team with three goals. Morocco have been led by Ismael Saibari, who has scored on three occasions in the current World Cup campaign, including two match-opening goals.

Both sides are in the midst of successful World Cup scoring streaks, as each have found the net in every match in the competition this season.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Canada have won three, drawn two and lost one. The Canadian side scored 12 and conceded four in that stretch, scoring first in four of the six contests. They had four first-half goals, while their opponents netted once in the first 45 minutes. The Moroccan side have won three and drawn three in their last six. The Moroccan side scored 12 and conceded five in that period, netting first in four of the six. Most of their goals (seven) came in the first half, while their opponents netted three times before the break.
