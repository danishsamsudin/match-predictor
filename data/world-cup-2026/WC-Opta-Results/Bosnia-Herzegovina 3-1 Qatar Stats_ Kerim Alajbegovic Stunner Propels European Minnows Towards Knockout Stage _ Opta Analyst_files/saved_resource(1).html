MATCH PREVIEW

Bosnia-Herzegovina are set to play Qatar on Wednesday at Seattle Stadium in the third round of the group stage at the 2026 Canada/Mexico/USA FIFA World Cup.

Bosnia suffered a first competition loss of the campaign following the 4-1 defeat in their last group stage match against Switzerland on June 18. Ermin Mahmic netted in the loss. They are winless in their last four games in all competitions. In six games this year, they have two wins, three draws, and one loss.

Qatar's last group stage game saw them beaten for the first time in the competition this year 6-0 by Canada on June 18 at BC Place Vancouver, extending their winless streak to four. In 2026, they have drawn two and lost two.

Bosnia-Herzegovina and Qatar are third and fourth in the group, respectively, level on one point. Bosnia are currently out of the knockout round places but still on for a place in the round of 32, while Qatar are in line to be eliminated.

Canada are atop the group on four points and are well-positioned to advance to the Round of 32. Switzerland are also in a Round of 32 spot, sitting second on four points.

The clubs played out a 1-1 draw in their last encounter in August 2010.

Jovo Lukic and Mahmic have caught the eye for Bosnia in World Cup play. They are the team's joint top scorers with one goal. Lukic has scored the critical first goal of the match once.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Bosnia have drawn five and lost one. The Bosnian side scored five and conceded eight over that period, scoring first in two of the six contests. They had two first-half goals, while their opponents netted two times in the first 45 minutes. The Qatari side have drawn two and lost three in their last six. The Qatari side scored one and conceded 11 in that period, failing to score first in any of the six. All of their goals came after halftime, while their opponents netted six times before the break.
