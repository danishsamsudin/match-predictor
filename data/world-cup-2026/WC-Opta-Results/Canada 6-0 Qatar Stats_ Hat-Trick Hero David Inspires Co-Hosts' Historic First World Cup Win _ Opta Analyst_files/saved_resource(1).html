MATCH PREVIEW

Two teams looking for their first win in the FIFA World Cup group stage will clash at BC Place Vancouver on Thursday, as Canada take on Qatar.

Canada played out a 1-1 draw in their opening World Cup group stage match against Bosnia-Herzegovina on June 12. Cyle Larin was the lone player on the scoresheet in the draw. They are unbeaten in their last nine games in all competitions.

Qatar played out a 1-1 draw against Switzerland in their opening World Cup game, a June 13 group stage clash at San Francisco Bay Area Stadium, stretching their winless streak to three. In 2026, they have drawn two and lost one.

Canada and Qatar are second and third in the group, respectively, level on one point. With one point, Switzerland are sitting atop the group. Bosnia-Herzegovina are fourth, also on one point.

When the two sides last met in action, Canada emerged victorious 2-0 in a friendly at Generali Arena in September 2022. Larin scored first for the Canadian side in the fourth minute. Jonathan David also was on target.

Larin leads Canada in World Cup scoring after netting against Bosnia-Herzegovina.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Canada have won two and drawn four. The Canadian side scored seven and conceded four in that stretch, scoring first in three of the six contests. They had one first-half goal, while their opponents netted three times in the first 45 minutes. Qatar have drawn three and lost two in their last six. The Qatari side scored two and conceded six in that period, netting first in one of the six. All of their goals came after halftime, while their opponents netted three times before the break.
