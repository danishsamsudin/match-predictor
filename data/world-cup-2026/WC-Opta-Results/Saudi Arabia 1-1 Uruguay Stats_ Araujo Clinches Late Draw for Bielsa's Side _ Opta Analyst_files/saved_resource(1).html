MATCH PREVIEW

Uruguay will be eager to halt a run of four straight winless matches as they take on Saudi Arabia in the FIFA World Cup group stage at Miami Stadium on Monday.

Both sides are playing their first match of the competition. Saudi Arabia played out a 0-0 draw in their last match, a June 9 friendly against Senegal at Toyota Field. In five games this year, they have one win, one draw, and three losses.

Uruguay played out a 0-0 draw against Algeria in their last game, a March 31 friendly at Allianz Stadium, stretching their winless streak to four games in all competitions.

The previous fixture between the sides saw Uruguay triumph 1-0 in a 2018 World Cup match at Rostov Arena in June 2018. Luis Suárez scored for the Uruguayan side in the 23rd minute.

Abdullah Al Hamddan and Sultan Mandash have made a difference in front of goal for Saudi Arabia in 2026. They are the team's joint top scorers with two goals apiece. Federico Valverde has been the only scorer for Uruguay this year, scoring once.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Saudi Arabia have won one, drawn one and lost four. The Saudi side scored five and conceded nine over that stretch, scoring first in two of the six contests. They had two first-half goals, while their opponents netted four times in the first 45 minutes. The Uruguayan side have won two, drawn three and lost one in their last six. The Uruguayan side scored five and conceded seven in that period, netting first in two of the six. They had only one goal in the first half, while their opponents netted four times before the break.
