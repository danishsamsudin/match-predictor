MATCH PREVIEW

Norway will look to extend their perfect start early in the FIFA World Cup group stage when they play Senegal at New York New Jersey Stadium on Monday.

Norway's opening World Cup group stage match saw them triumph 4-1 over Iraq on June 16. Erling Haaland caught the eye with two goals. They are unbeaten in their last four games in all competitions. In five games this year, they have two wins, two draws, and one loss.

Senegal suffered a 3-1 defeat against France in their opening World Cup group stage game, a June 16 clash at New York New Jersey Stadium, prolonging their winless streak to three. Ibrahim Mbaye was on the scoresheet in the loss. In 2026, they have won five, drawn one, and lost three.

Norway are atop the group on three points, while Senegal's zero points place them third. Meanwhile, France are sitting second on three points. Iraq are fourth on zero points.

Senegal won the previous encounter between the clubs, triumphing 2-1 in February 2006.

Haaland leads Norway in World Cup scoring after netting two goals against Iraq. With a goal against France, Mbaye is the early leading World Cup scorer for Senegal.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Norway have won three, drawn two and lost one. The Norwegian side scored 13 and conceded six in that period, scoring first in three of the six contests. They had six first-half goals, while their opponents netted four times in the first 45 minutes. The Senegalese side have won two, drawn one and lost three in their last six. The Senegalese side scored nine and conceded 10 over that period, netting first in three of the six. They had three goals in the first half, while their opponents netted two times before the break.
