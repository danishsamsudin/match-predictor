MATCH PREVIEW

Jordan take on Argentina for a FIFA World Cup group stage match at Dallas Stadium on Saturday with Argentina having already clinched a place in the Round of 32.

Jordan's last group stage match saw them beaten 2-1 by Algeria on June 22. Nizar Al Rashdan was on the scoresheet in the loss. They are winless in their last seven games in all competitions.

Argentina's last group stage game saw them triumph 2-0 over Austria on June 22 at Dallas Stadium, their ninth consecutive victory. Lionel Messi was the only player on the scoresheet, netting two goals.

Argentina lead the group on six points, while Jordan's zero points place them fourth. Argentina have clinched a place in the Round of 32, while Jordan are facing elimination.

Austria are second in the group on three points, well-positioned to advance to the Round of 32. Algeria are still in contention for a round of 32 place, sitting third on three points.

Al Rashdan and Ali Olwan have proved attacking threats for Jordan in World Cup play. The pair are the team's joint top scorers with one goal. Al Rashdan has scored the critical first goal of the match once. Argentina have been led by Messi, who has scored on five occasions in this season's World Cup, including two match-opening goals. The striker has found the net more than any other player in the competition.

Argentina have scored in each of their last nine contests in all competitions. They haven't been shut out since a World Cup Qualifiers meeting with Ecuador in September 2025. Argentina have been similarly successful at holding opponents scoreless, as they have shut out the opposition over their last five matches. Mauritania were the last team to score on them in a March friendly.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Jordan have drawn two and lost four. The Jordanian side scored seven and conceded 15 over that period, scoring first in three of the six contests. They had two first-half goals, while their opponents netted seven times in the first 45 minutes. The Argentinian side have won each of their last six.
