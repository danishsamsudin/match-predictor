MATCH PREVIEW

Scotland will look to extend their perfect start early in the FIFA World Cup group stage when they face Morocco at Boston Stadium on Friday.

Scotland's opening World Cup group stage match saw them triumph 1-0 over Haiti on June 13. John McGinn was the sole player to net in the win. They have three consecutive wins in all competitions. In five games this year, they have three wins and two losses.

Morocco played out a 1-1 draw against Brazil in their opening World Cup game, a June 13 group stage clash at New York New Jersey Stadium, improving their unbeaten streak to 30. Ismael Saibari was the sole player on the scoresheet in the draw.

Scotland are top of the group on three points, while Morocco's one point places them second. Elsewhere in the group, Brazil are sitting third on one point. Haiti are fourth on zero points.

The previous time the teams faced each other it was Morocco who clinched a 3-0 success in June 1998.

McGinn has the early World Cup scoring lead for Scotland after netting against Haiti. With a goal against Brazil, Saibari is the early leading World Cup scorer for Morocco.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Scotland have won four and lost two. The Scottish side scored 13 and conceded five over that period, scoring first in three of the six contests. A majority of their goals (seven) came in the first half, while their opponents netted two times in the first 45 minutes. The Moroccan side have won three and drawn three in their last six. The Moroccan side scored 14 and conceded four over that stretch, netting first in four of the six. They had four goals in the first half, while their opponents netted only once before the break.
