New Zealand take on Belgium on Friday at BC Place Vancouver in the third round of the group stage at the 2026 Canada/Mexico/USA FIFA World Cup.

New Zealand's last group stage match saw them beaten for the first time in the competition this season 3-1 by Egypt on June 21. Finn Surman was on the scoresheet in the loss. They are winless in their last four games in all competitions. In six games this year, they have one win, one draw, and four losses.

Belgium played out a 0-0 draw against IR Iran in their last game, a June 21 group stage clash at Los Angeles Stadium, extending their unbeaten streak to 15.

Belgium are third in the group on two points, while New Zealand's one point places them fourth. Belgium are currently out of the knockout round places but still on for a place in the round of 32, while New Zealand are facing elimination.

Atop the group on four points are Egypt, who are well-positioned to advance to the Round of 32. IR Iran are also in a Round of 32 spot, sitting second on two points.

In attack, Elijah Just has been a key player for New Zealand in World Cup play. He is the side's top scorer with two goals, one of those having been the tone-setting first of the match.

In their last six matches in all competitions, New Zealand have won one, drawn one and lost four. The New Zealander side scored seven and conceded 13 over that period, scoring first in three of the six contests. A majority of their goals (four) came in the first half, while their opponents netted four times in the first 45 minutes. The Belgian side have won three and drawn three in their last six.
