MATCH PREVIEW

Croatia play Ghana for a FIFA World Cup group stage match at Philadelphia Stadium on Saturday with Ghana looking to clinch a place in the Round of 32.

Croatia's last group stage match saw them triumph for the first time in the competition this season 1-0 over Panama on June 23. Ante Budimir was on the scoresheet in the win. In six games this year, they have three wins and three losses.

Ghana played out a 0-0 draw against England in their last game, a June 23 group stage clash at Boston Stadium, extending their unbeaten streak to three. In 2026, they have won one, drawn two, and lost three.

Ghana are second in the group on four points, while Croatia's three points place them third. Ghana are currently in line for the Round of 32, while Croatia are out of the knockout round places but still on for a place in the round of 32.

With four points, England are leading the group and are well-positioned to advance to the Round of 32. Panama are facing elimination, sitting fourth on zero points.

The trio of Martin Baturina, Petar Musa, and Budimir have provided stand-out moments for Croatia in World Cup play. They are the team's joint top scorers with one goal. Caleb Yirenkyi has been the most prolific player for Ghana, scoring once in this season's World Cup.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Croatia have won three and lost three. The Croatian side scored eight and conceded 11 in that period, scoring first in two of the six contests. They had four first-half goals, while their opponents netted five times in the first 45 minutes. Ghana have won one, drawn two and lost three in their last six.
