MATCH PREVIEW

Two teams looking for their first win in the FIFA World Cup group stage will clash at BC Place Vancouver on Sunday, as New Zealand face Egypt.

New Zealand played out a 2-2 draw in their opening World Cup group stage match against IR Iran on June 15. Elijah Just was the only player on the scoresheet, netting two goals. They are winless in their last three games in all competitions. In five games this year, they have one win, one draw, and three losses.

Egypt played out a 1-1 draw against Belgium in their opening World Cup game, a June 15 group stage clash at Seattle Stadium. Emam Ashour scored in the draw. In 2026, they have won four, drawn two, and lost three.

New Zealand and Egypt are first and fourth in the group, respectively, level on one point. Meanwhile, IR Iran are sitting second on one point. Belgium are third on one point.

When the two sides last met in action, Egypt triumphed 1-0 in a friendly at New Administrative Capital Stadium in March 2024. Mostafa Mohamed scored for the Egyptian side in the 29th minute with a spot-kick.

Just leads New Zealand in World Cup scoring after netting a brace against IR Iran. With a goal against Belgium, Ashour is the early leading World Cup scorer for Egypt.

In their last six matches in all competitions, New Zealand have won one, drawn one and lost four. The New Zealander side scored six and conceded 12 in that period, scoring first in two of the six contests. They had three first-half goals, while their opponents netted four times in the first 45 minutes. The Egyptian side have won two, drawn two and lost two in their last six. The Egyptian side scored seven and conceded three over that stretch, netting first in three of the six. Most of their goals (five) came in the first half, while their opponents netted only once before the break.
