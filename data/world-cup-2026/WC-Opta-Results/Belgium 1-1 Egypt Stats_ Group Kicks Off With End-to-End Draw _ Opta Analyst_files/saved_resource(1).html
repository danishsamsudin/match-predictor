Egypt face an in-form Belgium side who have not suffered defeat in 13 matches for a FIFA World Cup group stage clash on Monday at Seattle Stadium.

It's the first match of the competition for both sides. Belgium's last match saw them triumph 5-0 over Tunisia in a friendly at Stade Roi Baudouin on June 6. Five players netted in the win.

Egypt were defeated in their last game, falling 2-1 against Brazil in a friendly at Huntington Bank Field on June 6. Mostafa Zico was the only player to net in the loss. In 2026, they have won four, drawn one, and lost three.

When the two sides last met in action, Egypt won 2-1 in a friendly at Jaber Al-Ahmad International Stadium in November 2022. Mostafa Mohamed scored first for the Egyptian side in the 33rd minute. Trezeguet also was on target. Loïs Openda scored Belgium's only goal in the 76th minute.

In attack, Dodi Lukébakio has been a key performer this year for Belgium. He is the side's leading scorer with four goals. Egypt have been led this year by Zico, Omar Marmoush, and Mohamed Salah, who have each netted twice.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Belgium have won four and drawn two. The Belgian side scored 21 and conceded four over that period, scoring first in three of the six contests. They had six first-half goals, while their opponents netted three times in the first 45 minutes. The Egyptian side have won two, drawn one and lost three in their last six.
