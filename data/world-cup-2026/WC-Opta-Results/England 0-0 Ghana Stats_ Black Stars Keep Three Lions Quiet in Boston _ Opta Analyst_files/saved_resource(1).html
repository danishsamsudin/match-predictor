MATCH PREVIEW

England face Ghana on Tuesday at Boston Stadium in a clash of teams who boast perfect starts early in the FIFA World Cup group stage.

England defeated Croatia 4-2 in their opening World Cup group stage match on June 17. Harry Kane netted two to lead the scoring. They have three consecutive wins in all competitions. In five games this year, they have three wins, one draw, and one loss.

Ghana's opening World Cup group stage game saw them triumph 1-0 over Panama on June 17 at Toronto Stadium. Caleb Yirenkyi was on the scoresheet in the win. In 2026, they have won one, drawn one, and lost three.

England and Ghana are first and second in the group, respectively, level on three points. Meanwhile, Panama are sitting third on zero points. Croatia are fourth on zero points.

The teams played out a 1-1 draw in their previous encounter in March 2011.

Kane has the early World Cup scoring lead for England after netting two goals against Croatia. With a goal against Panama, Yirenkyi is the early leading World Cup scorer for Ghana.

In their last six matches in all competitions, England have won four, drawn one and lost one. The English side scored 11 and conceded four over that stretch, scoring first in five of the six contests. They had four first-half goals, while their opponents netted three times in the first 45 minutes. The Ghanaian side have won one, drawn one and lost four in their last six. The Ghanaian side scored four and conceded 11 over that period, netting first in two of the six. All of their goals came after halftime, while their opponents netted three times before the break.
