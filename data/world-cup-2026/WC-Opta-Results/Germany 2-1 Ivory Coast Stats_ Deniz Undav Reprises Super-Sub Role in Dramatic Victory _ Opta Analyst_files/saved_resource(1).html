MATCH PREVIEW

Germany face Côte d'Ivoire on Saturday at Toronto Stadium in a clash of teams who are yet to drop points early in the FIFA World Cup group stage.

Germany's opening World Cup group stage match saw them triumph 7-1 over Curaçao on June 14. Kai Havertz netted a brace to lead the scoring. They have 10 consecutive wins in all competitions.

Côte d'Ivoire won their opening World Cup group stage game on June 14, beating Ecuador 1-0 at Philadelphia Stadium, their fourth consecutive victory. Amad Diallo was the sole player on the scoresheet in the win. In 2026, they have won five and lost one.

Germany and Côte d'Ivoire are first and second in the group, respectively, level on three points. Meanwhile, Ecuador are sitting third on zero points. Curaçao are fourth on zero points.

The clubs played out a 2-2 draw in their last encounter in November 2009.

Havertz has the early World Cup scoring lead for Germany after netting two goals against Curaçao. With a goal against Ecuador, Diallo is the early leading World Cup scorer for Côte d'Ivoire.

Both clubs have been difficult to stop in attack recently, as each have been on the scoresheet in their last 10 games in all competitions.

Germany have won each of their last six matches in all competitions. The German side scored 25 and conceded six in that period, scoring first in five of the six contests. They had 12 first-half goals, while their opponents netted four times in the first 45 minutes. Côte d'Ivoire have won five and lost one in their last six. The Ivorian side scored 13 and conceded four in that period, netting first in four of the six. They had six goals in the first half, while their opponents netted three times before the break.
