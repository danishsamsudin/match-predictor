MATCH PREVIEW

Uruguay play Spain on Friday at Estadio Guadalajara for a FIFA World Cup group stage match between two in-form teams looking to clinch a place in the Round of 32.

Uruguay played out a 2-2 draw in their last group stage match against Cabo Verde on June 21. Maxi Araújo and Agustín Canobbio scored in the draw. They are winless in their last six games in all competitions.

Spain's last group stage game saw them triumph for the first time in the competition this year, winning 4-0 over Saudi Arabia on June 21 at Atlanta Stadium, improving their unbeaten streak to 12. Mikel Oyarzabal netted two to lead the scoring.

Spain are top of the group on four points, while Uruguay's two points place them second. Both sides are currently in position for a place in the Round of 32.

Elsewhere in the group, Cabo Verde are third on two points and still in contention for a round of 32 place. Saudi Arabia are facing elimination, sitting fourth on one point.

The last time the teams came up against each other it was Spain who secured a 2-1 victory in June 2013.

Uruguay have been led by Araújo in World Cup play. He leads the team with two goals. Oyarzabal has been the leading attacking threat for Spain, scoring twice in this World Cup campaign.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Uruguay have drawn five and lost one. The Uruguayan side scored five and conceded nine over that period, failing to score first in any of the six contests. A majority of their goals (three) came in the first half, while their opponents netted six times in the first 45 minutes. The Spanish side have won three and drawn three in their last six. The Spanish side scored 11 and conceded two over that stretch, netting first in four of the six. Most of their goals (eight) came in the first half, while their opponents netted only once before the break.
