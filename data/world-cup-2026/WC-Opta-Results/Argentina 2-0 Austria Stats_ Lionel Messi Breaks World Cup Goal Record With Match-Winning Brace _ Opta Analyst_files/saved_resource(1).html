MATCH PREVIEW

Argentina play Austria on Monday at Dallas Stadium in a clash of teams who boast perfect starts early in the FIFA World Cup group stage.

Argentina earned a 3-0 victory over Algeria in their opening World Cup group stage match on June 16. Lionel Messi was the only player on the scoresheet, netting three goals. They have eight consecutive victories in all competitions.

Austria defeated Jordan 3-1 in their opening World Cup group stage game, a June 16 clash at San Francisco Bay Area Stadium, extending their unbeaten streak to six. Romano Schmid and Marko Arnautovic were on the scoresheet in the win.

Argentina and Austria are first and second in the group, respectively, level on three points. Elsewhere in the group, Jordan are sitting third on zero points. Algeria are fourth on zero points.

The teams played out a 1-1 draw in their last meeting in May 1990.

Messi has the early World Cup scoring lead for Argentina after netting three goals against Algeria. With goals against Jordan, Schmid and Arnautovic are the early leading World Cup scorers for Austria.

Argentina have scored in each of their last eight matches across all competitions. They have netted in every match since a World Cup Qualifiers meeting with Ecuador in September 2025. The Argentinian side have been similarly successful at preventing opponents from scoring, as they have shut out the opposition over their last 356 minutes of play. Mauritania were the last team to score on them in a March friendly.

Argentina have won each of their last six matches in all competitions. The Argentinian side scored 17 and conceded one over that stretch, scoring first in all six contests. They had eight first-half goals, while their opponents failed to find the net in the first 45 minutes. The Austrian side have won five and drawn one in their last six. The Austrian side scored 13 and conceded three over that period, netting first in five of the six. They had three goals in the first half, while their opponents netted one time before the break.
