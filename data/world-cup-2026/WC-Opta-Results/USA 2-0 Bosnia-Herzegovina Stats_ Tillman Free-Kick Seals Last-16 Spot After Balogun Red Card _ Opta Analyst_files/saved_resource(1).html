The United States face Bosnia-Herzegovina on Wednesday for a FIFA World Cup Round of 32 clash at San Francisco Bay Area Stadium.

The United States' last group stage match saw them beaten for the first time in the competition this season 3-2 by Türkiye on June 25. Auston Trusty and Sebastian Berhalter scored in the loss. In seven games this year, they have three wins and four losses.

Bosnia earned a first win in the competition this year with a 3-1 victory over Qatar in their last group stage game, a June 24 clash at Seattle Stadium. Kerim Alajbegovic and Ermin Mahmic scored in the win. In 2026, they have won three, drawn three, and lost one.

The United States finished first in their group on six points, while Bosnia's four points placed them third.

The previous fixture between the sides saw the United States win 1-0 in a friendly at Dignity Health Sports Park in December 2021. Cole Bassett scored for the USA side in the 89th minute.

In attack, Folarin Balogun has been a key performer for the United States in World Cup play. The attacker is the team's top scorer with two goals. Bosnia have been led by Mahmic, who has scored twice in the current World Cup campaign.

Both sides are in the midst of successful World Cup scoring streaks, as each have found the net in every match in the competition this season.

In their last six matches in all competitions, the United States have won three and lost three. The USA side scored 12 and conceded 10 over that period, scoring first in four of the six contests. A majority of their goals (nine) came in the first half, while their opponents netted five times in the first 45 minutes. The Bosnian side have won one, drawn four and lost one in their last six. The Bosnian side scored seven and conceded eight over that stretch, netting first in three of the six.
