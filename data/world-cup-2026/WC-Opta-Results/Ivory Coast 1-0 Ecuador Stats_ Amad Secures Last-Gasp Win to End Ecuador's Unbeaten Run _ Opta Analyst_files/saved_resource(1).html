MATCH PREVIEW

Côte d'Ivoire and Ecuador face off in the FIFA World Cup group stage at Philadelphia Stadium on Sunday with both sides in the midst of successful streaks, as Côte d'Ivoire are looking for their fourth straight victory while Ecuador are unbeaten in their last 19.

It's the first match of the competition for both sides. Côte d'Ivoire earned a 2-1 victory over France in their last match, a June 4 friendly at Stade de la Beaujoire - Louis Fonteneau, their third victory in a row. Guéla Doué and Amad Diallo netted in the win. In five games this year, they have four wins and one loss.

Ecuador's last game saw them triumph 3-0 over Guatemala in a friendly on June 7 at ScottsMiracle-Gro Field. Jordy Caicedo, Nilson Angulo, and Pervis Estupiñán scored in the win.

Doué and Diallo have made a difference in front of goal for Côte d'Ivoire this year. The two players are the team's joint top scorers with two goals apiece. Ecuador have been led by seven players who have each scored once this year.

Côte d'Ivoire have scored in nine straight contests across all competitions. They haven't been shut out since a friendly with Saudi Arabia in November 2025.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Côte d'Ivoire have won five and lost one. The Ivorian side scored 15 and conceded six in that period, scoring first in three of the six contests. They had seven first-half goals, while their opponents netted five times in the first 45 minutes. The Ecuadorean side have won three and drawn three in their last six. The Ecuadorean side scored nine and conceded three over that stretch, netting first in four of the six. They had three goals in the first half, while their opponents netted only once before the break.
