MATCH PREVIEW

Ecuador face Curaçao on Saturday at Kansas City Stadium in a meeting of teams who have yet to earn a win early in the FIFA World Cup group stage.

Ecuador slumped 1-0 against Côte d'Ivoire in their opening World Cup group stage match on June 14. In five games this year, they have two wins, two draws, and one loss.

Curaçao suffered a 7-1 defeat against Germany in their opening World Cup group stage game, a June 14 clash at Houston Stadium. Livano Comenencia was the only player to net in the loss. In 2026, they have won one and lost four.

Ecuador and Curaçao are third and fourth in the group, respectively, level on zero points. Germany are sitting atop the group on three points. Côte d'Ivoire are second, also on three points.

Ecuador have yet to find the net in the World Cup this season. With a goal against Germany, Comenencia is the early leading World Cup scorer for Curaçao.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Ecuador have won three, drawn two and lost one. The Ecuadorean side scored nine and conceded four over that stretch, scoring first in four of the six contests. They had three first-half goals, while their opponents netted once in the first 45 minutes. Curaçao have won one, drawn one and lost four in their last six. The Curaçao side scored seven and conceded 18 over that period, netting first in two of the six. They had two goals in the first half, while their opponents netted six times before the break.
