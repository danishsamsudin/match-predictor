MATCH PREVIEW

Ecuador face Germany for a FIFA World Cup group stage match at New York New Jersey Stadium on Thursday with the German side having already clinched a place in the Round of 32.

Ecuador played out a 0-0 draw in their last group stage match against Curaçao on June 20 to earn their first point in the competition this season. In six games this year, they have two wins, three draws, and one loss.

Germany won their last group stage game on June 20, beating Côte d'Ivoire 2-1 at Toronto Stadium, their 11th consecutive victory. Deniz Undav was the only player on the scoresheet, netting a brace.

Germany lead the group on six points. Ecuador's one point places them third, currently out of the knockout round places but still on for a place in the round of 32.

With three points, Côte d'Ivoire are second in the group, well-positioned to advance to the Round of 32. Curaçao are facing elimination, sitting fourth on one point.

Germany won the previous meeting between the clubs, triumphing 4-2 in May 2013.

Ecuador have yet to find the target in World Cup play this season. Undav has been the leading attacking threat for Germany, scoring on three occasions in this World Cup campaign. The attacker is tied at the top of the scoring charts in the competition.

Germany have scored in each of their last 11 games across all competitions. They have hit the back of the net in every match since a World Cup Qualifiers meeting with Slovakia in September 2025. The Ecuadorean side, meanwhile, are scoreless in their last 192 minutes of play. They last found the net against Guatemala in a 7 June friendly.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Ecuador have won two, drawn three and lost one. The Ecuadorean side scored seven and conceded four in that stretch, scoring first in three of the six contests. They had three first-half goals, while their opponents netted once in the first 45 minutes. The German side have won each of their last six. The German side scored 21 and conceded seven in that period, netting first in four of the six. They had eight goals in the first half, while their opponents netted five times before the break.
