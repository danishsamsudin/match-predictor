MATCH PREVIEW

Spain take on Austria on Thursday for a FIFA World Cup Round of 32 clash at Los Angeles Stadium.

Spain earned a 1-0 victory over Uruguay in their last group stage match on June 26. Álex Baena was the only player on the scoresheet in the win. They are undefeated in their last 13 games in all competitions.

Austria played out a 3-3 draw against Algeria in their last game, a June 27 group stage clash at Kansas City Stadium. Marko Arnautovic, Marcel Sabitzer, and Sasa Kalajdzic netted in the draw. In 2026, they have won four, drawn one, and lost one.

Spain finished first in their group on seven points, while Austria's four points placed them second.

The previous encounter between the teams saw Spain emerge victorious 5-1 in November 2009.

Spain have been led by Mikel Oyarzabal in World Cup play. The striker leads the team with two goals. Austria's most productive player in attack has been Arnautovic, who has scored twice in this World Cup campaign, including one match-opening goal.

The Spanish side have been difficult to score on in World Cup play, as they haven't allowed a goal in the competition this season

In their last six matches in all competitions, Spain have won three and drawn three. The Spanish side scored nine and conceded two in that period, scoring first in four of the six contests. A majority of their goals (seven) came in the first half, while their opponents netted once in the first 45 minutes. The Austrian side have won four, drawn one and lost one in their last six.
