MATCH PREVIEW

England take on Congo DR on Wednesday for a FIFA World Cup Round of 32 clash at Atlanta Stadium.

England earned a 2-0 victory over Panama in their last group stage match on June 27. Jude Bellingham and Harry Kane were on the scoresheet in the win. They are unbeaten in their last five games in all competitions. In seven games this year, they have four wins, two draws, and one loss.

Congo DR earned a first win in the competition this year with a 3-1 victory over Uzbekistan in their last group stage game, a June 27 clash at Atlanta Stadium. Yoane Wissa netted a brace to lead the scoring. In 2026, they have won three, drawn two, and lost three.

England finished first in their group on seven points, while Congo DR's four points placed them third.

Kane has caught the eye for England in World Cup play. He leads the team with three goals, one of those having been the crucial first of the match. Wissa has been the most prolific player for Congo DR, scoring on three occasions in this World Cup campaign.

The English side have been impressive in defence in recent World Cup play, as they have shut out opponents over their last 220 minutes of play this season, dating to a Petar Musa finish in their group stage win against Croatia

In their last six matches in all competitions, England have won four, drawn one and lost one. The English side scored 10 and conceded three over that period, scoring first in four of the six contests. They had four first-half goals, while their opponents netted three times in the first 45 minutes. The Congo DR side have won two, drawn two and lost two in their last six. The Congo DR side scored six and conceded five over that period, netting first in one of the six. They had only one goal in the first half, while their opponents netted two times before the break.
