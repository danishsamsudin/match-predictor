MATCH PREVIEW

Austria and Jordan face off in the FIFA World Cup group stage at San Francisco Bay Area Stadium on Tuesday as two sides on opposite paths with Austria hoping to build on a five-match unbeaten streak and Jordan winless in five straight.

Both sides are playing their first match of the competition. Austria's last match saw them triumph 1-0 over Tunisia in a friendly at Ernst-Happel-Stadion on June 1. Marcel Sabitzer was the lone player on the scoresheet in the win.

Jordan slumped 2-0 against Colombia in their last game, a June 7 friendly at Snapdragon Stadium.

Sabitzer has caught the eye in 2026 for Austria. He leads the side with three goals. With one goal each in 2026, five players share the scoring lead for Jordan.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Austria have won four, drawn one and lost one. The Austrian side scored 10 and conceded three in that period, scoring first in four of the six contests. They had two first-half goals, while their opponents netted once in the first 45 minutes. The Jordanian side have won one, drawn two and lost three in their last six. The Jordanian side scored eight and conceded 13 in that stretch, netting first in three of the six. They had only one goal in the first half, while their opponents netted seven times before the break.
