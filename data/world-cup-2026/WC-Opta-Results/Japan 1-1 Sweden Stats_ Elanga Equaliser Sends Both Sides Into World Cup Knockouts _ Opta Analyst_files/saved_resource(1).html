Sweden play Japan for a FIFA World Cup group stage match at Dallas Stadium on Thursday with the Japanese side looking to clinch a place in the Round of 32.

Yasin Ayari has been the leading attacking threat for Sweden, netting twice in this World Cup campaign, including one match-opening goal. He is tied for fourth in the competition's scoring charts.
