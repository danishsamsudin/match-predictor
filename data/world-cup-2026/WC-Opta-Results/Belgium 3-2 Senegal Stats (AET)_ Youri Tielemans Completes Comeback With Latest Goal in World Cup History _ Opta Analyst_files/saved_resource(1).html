MATCH PREVIEW

Belgium take on Senegal on Wednesday for a FIFA World Cup Round of 32 clash at Seattle Stadium.

Belgium earned a first competition win this season with a 5-1 victory over New Zealand in their last group stage match on June 26. Leandro Trossard caught the eye with two goals. They are undefeated in their last 16 games in all competitions.
