MATCH PREVIEW

Australia face an in-form Türkiye side who have not suffered defeat in eight matches on Saturday in a FIFA World Cup group stage clash at BC Place Vancouver in Vancouver, British Columbia.

Both sides are playing their first match of the competition. Australia played out a 1-1 draw in their last match, a June 6 friendly against Switzerland at Snapdragon Stadium. Tete Yengi was the sole player to score in the draw. In four games this year, they have two wins, one draw, and one loss.

Türkiye won their last game on June 6, beating Venezuela 2-1 in a friendly at Chase Stadium. Baris Alper Yilmaz and Yunus Akgün scored in the win.

The previous time the clubs played each other it was Türkiye who claimed a 1-0 success in May 2004.

The duo of Nestory Irankunda and Jordan Bos have provided stand-out moments for Australia this year. The two players are the team's joint top scorers with two goals apiece. Yilmaz has been the most prolific player for Türkiye in 2026, scoring twice.

Türkiye have scored in eight straight contests in all competitions. They have been on the scoresheet ever since a World Cup Qualifiers meeting with Spain in September 2025.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Australia have won two, drawn one and lost three. The Australian side scored seven and conceded seven over that stretch, scoring first in two of the six contests. They had one first-half goal, while their opponents netted three times in the first 45 minutes. Türkiye have won five and drawn one in their last six. The Türkiye side scored 12 and conceded three in that period, netting first in four of the six. They had five goals in the first half, while their opponents netted two times before the break.
