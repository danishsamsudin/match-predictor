MATCH PREVIEW

Türkiye play Paraguay on Friday at San Francisco Bay Area Stadium in a clash of teams who are looking for their first win of the FIFA World Cup group stage.

Türkiye slumped 2-0 against Australia in their opening World Cup group stage match on June 13. In five games this year, they have four wins and one loss.

Paraguay were defeated in their opening World Cup group stage game, falling 4-1 against the United States at Los Angeles Stadium on June 12. Mauricio was the sole player on the scoresheet in the loss. In 2026, they have won two and lost two.

Türkiye and Paraguay are third and fourth in the group, respectively, level on zero points. The United States are leading the group on three points. Australia are second, also on three points.

The clubs played out a 0-0 draw in their previous encounter in June 1995.

Türkiye have yet to score in the World Cup this season. With a goal against the United States, Mauricio is the early leading World Cup scorer for Paraguay.
