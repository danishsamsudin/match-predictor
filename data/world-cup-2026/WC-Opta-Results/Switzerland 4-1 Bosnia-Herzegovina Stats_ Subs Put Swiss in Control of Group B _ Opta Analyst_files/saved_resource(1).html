MATCH PREVIEW

Switzerland face Bosnia-Herzegovina on Thursday at Los Angeles Stadium in a clash of teams who are looking for their first win of the FIFA World Cup group stage.

Switzerland earned a point in their opening World Cup group stage match, drawing 1-1 against Qatar on June 13. Breel Embolo was the lone player on the scoresheet in the draw. They are unbeaten in their last four games in all competitions. In five games this year, they have one win, three draws, and one loss.

Bosnia played out a 1-1 draw against Canada in their opening World Cup game, a June 12 group stage clash at Toronto Stadium, improving their unbeaten streak to nine. Jovo Lukic was the lone player to score in the draw.

Switzerland and Bosnia-Herzegovina are first and fourth in the group, respectively, level on one point. Elsewhere in the group, Canada are sitting second on one point. Qatar are third on one point.

The previous fixture between the teams saw Bosnia triumph 2-0 in a friendly at Stadion Letzigrund in March 2016. Edin Dzeko scored first for the Bosnian side in the 14th minute. Miralem Pjanic also was on target.

Embolo leads Switzerland in World Cup scoring after netting against Qatar. With a goal against Canada, Lukic is the early leading World Cup scorer for Bosnia.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Switzerland have won one, drawn four and lost one. The Swiss side scored 10 and conceded eight over that period, scoring first in five of the six contests. A majority of their goals (seven) came in the first half, while their opponents netted two times in the first 45 minutes. Bosnia and Herzegovina have drawn each of their last six. The Bosnian side scored five and conceded five over that period, netting first in three of the six. Most of their goals (three) came in the first half, while their opponents netted two times before the break.
