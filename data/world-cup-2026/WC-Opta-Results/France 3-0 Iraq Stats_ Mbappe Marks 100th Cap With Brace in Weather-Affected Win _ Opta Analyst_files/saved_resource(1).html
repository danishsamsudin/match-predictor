France will look to protect their perfect start early in the FIFA World Cup group stage when they take on Iraq at Philadelphia Stadium on Monday.

Mbappé leads France in World Cup scoring after netting two goals against Senegal. With a goal against Norway, Hussein is the early leading World Cup scorer for Iraq.

France have scored in each of their last 14 contests across all competitions. They have netted in every match since a Nations League meeting with Croatia in March 2025.
