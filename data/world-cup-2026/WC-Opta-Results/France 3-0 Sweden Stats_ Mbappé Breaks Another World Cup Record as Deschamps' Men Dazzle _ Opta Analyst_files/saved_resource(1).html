MATCH PREVIEW

Continuing a strong run of FIFA World Cup form will be the aim for France when they face Sweden on Tuesday at New York New Jersey Stadium in the Round of 32.

France's last group stage match saw them triumph 4-1 over Norway on June 26. Ousmane Dembélé led the team with a hat-trick. They have four consecutive wins in all competitions. In seven games this year, they have six wins and one loss.

Sweden played out a 1-1 draw against Japan in their last game, a June 25 group stage clash at Dallas Stadium. Anthony Elanga was the lone player to net in the draw. In 2026, they have won three, drawn two, and lost two.

France finished first in their group on nine points, while Sweden's four points placed them third.

The previous fixture between the teams saw France win 4-2 in an UEFA Nations League League A match at Stade de France in November 2020. Olivier Giroud led the French side with two goals, netting in the 16th and 59th minute. Benjamin Pavard and Kingsley Coman also were on the scoresheet. Viktor Claesson and Robin Quaison were on the scoresheet for the Swedish side.

Dembélé and Kylian Mbappé have caught the eye for France in World Cup play. The pair are the team's joint top scorers with four goals, tied for the second highest in the competition's scoring charts. Mbappé has scored the crucial first goal of the match two times, while Dembélé has one such strike. Sweden's leading players in the final third have been Yasin Ayari and Elanga, who have each scored twice in this World Cup campaign.

Both sides have been difficult to stop in attack in World Cup play, as each have found the net in every match in the competition this season.

In their last six matches in all competitions, France have won five and lost one. The French side scored 17 and conceded six in that stretch, scoring first in all six contests. They had eight first-half goals, while their opponents netted once in the first 45 minutes. The Swedish side have won two, drawn two and lost two in their last six. The Swedish side scored 13 and conceded 14 in that stretch, netting first in two of the six. They had four goals in the first half, while their opponents netted eight times before the break.
