MATCH PREVIEW

Switzerland play Canada on Wednesday at BC Place Vancouver for a FIFA World Cup group stage match between two in-form teams looking to secure a place in the Round of 32.

Switzerland earned a first competition win this season with a 4-1 victory over Bosnia-Herzegovina in their last group stage match on June 18. Johan Manzambi netted a brace to lead the scoring. They are undefeated in their last five games in all competitions. In six games this year, they have two wins, three draws, and one loss.

Canada won for the first time in the competition this year in their last group stage game on June 18, beating Qatar 6-0 at BC Place Vancouver, improving their unbeaten streak to 10. Jonathan David netted a hat-trick to lead the scoring.

Canada and Switzerland are first and second in the group, respectively, level on four points. Both sides are currently in position for a place in the Round of 32.

Elsewhere in the group, Bosnia-Herzegovina are third on one point and still in contention for a round of 32 place. Qatar are in danger of elimination, sitting fourth on one point.

The previous time the clubs faced each other it was Canada who claimed a 3-1 success in May 2002.

Manzambi has caught the eye for Switzerland in World Cup play. The midfielder is their top scorer with two goals, which is tied for the fourth highest in the competition, one of those having been the critical first of the match. Canada have been led by David, who has scored on three occasions in this World Cup campaign. He is tied as the competition's top scorer.

In their last six matches in all competitions, Switzerland have won two, drawn three and lost one. The Swiss side scored 13 and conceded eight in that stretch, scoring first in five of the six contests. A majority of their goals (seven) came in the first half, while their opponents netted two times in the first 45 minutes. The Canadian side have won two and drawn four in their last six. The Canadian side scored 12 and conceded four in that stretch, netting first in three of the six. They had four goals in the first half, while their opponents netted three times before the break.
