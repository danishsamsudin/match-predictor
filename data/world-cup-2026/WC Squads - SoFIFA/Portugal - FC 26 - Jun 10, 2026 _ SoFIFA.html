It really triggers me a lot every time I hear people say that this is Portugal’s best-ever squad - including some Portuguese people. The 1966 World Cup team had a very experienced defence (especially Germano, José Pereira, Vicente Lucas, João Morais, Alexandre Baptista, Hilário), and then some of the best midfielders (Mário Coluna, Jaime Graça, Fernando Cruz) and attackers (Eusébio, José Torres, António Simões, José Augusto) in the country’s history, many being from Benfica, the best team in the world in the 60s.

Then, both the Euro 2004 and the World Cup 2006 teams are star struck, with good options in every position on the field, and a great backbone from the Porto team which won the 2003 UEFA Cup and the 2004 Champions League back to back. We had GKs like Ricardo and Quim; defenders like Ricardo Carvalho, Paulo Ferreira, Jorge Andrade, Ricardo Costa, Nuno Valente and Miguel; midfielders like Luís Figo, Deco, Costinha, Maniche, Tiago Mendes and Petit; attackers like young Cristiano Ronaldo, Pauleta, Nuno Gomes, Simão Sabrosa and Hélder Postiga. Maybe some were not big international stars but all were very solid players who complemented each other well.

On the other hand, this current 2026 squad struggles to find even one good option for striker or for centre-back. Also, other than the midfield, this team doesn’t truly play as a unit, and it’s clear in, for instance, how many crosses go to no man’s land in the attack.
